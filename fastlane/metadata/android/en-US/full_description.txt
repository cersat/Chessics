Chessics is a chess app where everything runs on your phone.

<b>Opponents</b>
<ul>
<li>Stockfish 17.1 on device: 1350 to 3000 Elo and full strength</li>
<li>Own bots: "Material" (search, counts material only) and "Algorithm" (rules, no search)</li>
<li>Two players on one phone: face to face or pass-and-play, with a chess clock</li>
<li>Any player for each side, even bot vs bot</li>
<li>Online via lichess.org: random opponents, challenge a friend, server Stockfish (your own lichess account is required)</li>
</ul>

<b>Review</b>
<ul>
<li>Stockfish evaluation bar</li>
<li>Game review with move classification: brilliant, great, best, inaccuracy, mistake, blunder</li>
<li>Accuracy, evaluation graph, PGN export</li>
</ul>

<b>Puzzles</b>
<ul>
<li>Hundreds of puzzles from the open lichess database by theme: mate in 1–4, fork, skewer, pin, discovered attack, sacrifice and more</li>
<li>Daily puzzle and a generator of new puzzles on a chosen theme</li>
</ul>

<b>Progress</b>
<ul>
<li>Daily streak with streak freezes</li>
<li>Diamonds for wins, puzzles and achievements</li>
<li>Shop: themes (dark and light), boards, piece sets, widget skins</li>
<li>Streak and daily puzzle widgets, evening reminder</li>
</ul>

No ads, no trackers, no in-app purchases for money.

The interface is in Russian.
